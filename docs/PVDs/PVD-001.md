# Project Vision Document (PVD-001)

## Why CSE Exists

The Claire Speech Engine (CSE) exists to provide a production-grade, highly performant, and stable foundation for expressive speech synthesis. Most speech research focuses strictly on raw acoustic generation without modeling communicative intent. CSE solves this by modeling human communication before generating human speech, strictly separating communication reasoning from acoustic synthesis.

## Project Philosophy

1. **Layer Separation**: CSE strictly separates engine orchestration, CPE communication reasoning, and CAM acoustic realization.
2. **Minimal and Lazy**: We favor deletion over addition. Features are only added when absolutely necessary. There is no bloat, no speculative abstractions, and no "just in case" architecture.
3. **Immutability and Pure Contracts**: `PerformanceGraph`, audio frames, and configuration payloads are immutable, ensuring thread-safe data flow.

## The PerformanceGraph Contract

CPE defines speech performance once as an immutable `PerformanceGraph`. The native synthesis target is CAM (Claire Acoustic Model). Compatible acoustic implementations (such as KittenTTS) may consume the `PerformanceGraph` via translation when satisfying the CAM contract, keeping higher-level application logic independent of acoustic renderer details.

## Performance Before Acoustics

Expressive reasoning is ineffective if the engine cannot deliver audio in real time. CSE was designed with strict performance constraints enforced by automated benchmarking:
- Initialization under 20ms
- Package import under 50ms
- Backend lookup under 10ms

We built high-performance streaming audio transport (`cse.streaming`) and deterministic reasoning pipelines (`cse.performance`) to guarantee that the engine's overhead is minimal.

## Current Architecture & Roadmap

In **v1.0.5**, the project operates across three dedicated layers:

1. **CSE (Claire Speech Engine)**: The complete speech engine framework, runtime lifecycle, and transport orchestration.
2. **CPE (Claire Performance Engine)**: The communication intelligence layer — currently running deterministic reasoning passes (`meaning`, `intent`, `planning`) that construct canonical `PerformanceGraph` instances.
3. **CAM (Claire Acoustic Model)**: The native acoustic model of the project (with KittenTTS actively serving as the current compatible acoustic implementation satisfying the `PerformanceGraph` contract).

This separation of concerns allows continuous enhancement of communication intelligence without destabilizing engine infrastructure.
